# Cross-Year Comparison: Proposed vs Adopted — State Audit

**Date:** 2026-03-15
**Purpose:** Assess what cross-year comparison capabilities exist, what data is available for deeper analysis, and what gaps remain before expanding the feature.

## Current Comparison Page

The existing page at `/cross-year/` compares **department-level budget totals** for 2025:
- "2025 Proposed" from the 2025 budget document (mayor's proposal)
- "2025 Adopted" from the 2026 budget document (council-approved)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Departments compared | 35 |
| Unchanged | 31 |
| Changed by council | 4 |
| Net change (dept-level) | +$20.2M |

The 4 changed departments:
- Bureau of Emergency Medical Services: +$11.3M (+41%)
- Bureau of Facilities: +$7.6M (+173%)
- Bureau of Administration (Public Safety): +$1.4M (+11.2%)
- Mellon Park Trust Fund: small increase

### What the page does well
- Summary cards, sortable table, Observable Plot bar chart
- Data quality filter (>10x outlier exclusion)
- Links to department detail pages
- Prominent placement in main nav

### What it doesn't show
- Line-item or sub-department differences
- Personnel/FTE changes
- Capital project changes
- Revenue assumption changes

## Aggregate-Level Gap

The department-level comparison shows +$20.2M, but the `budget-changes.json` data reveals:

| Metric | Proposed (2025 doc) | Adopted (2026 doc) | Difference |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|------------|
| Total budget | $465.8M | $491.5M | **+$25.7M** |
| Total FTE | 2,761 | 2,904 | **+143** |

The $5.5M gap between the page's +$20.2M and the actual +$25.7M comes from differences that are visible at sub-department or line-item level but wash out in department-level aggregation.

## Data Available for Deeper Analysis

### Personnel (position-level)

Both years have individual position records (`personnel.json`):

| Metric | 2025 doc | 2026 doc |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Position records | 923 | 974 |
| Positions in both | 756 (matched by dept+title) |
| Only in proposed | 110 |
| Only in adopted | 158 |

Key findings:
- **Bureau of Environmental Services** gained 203 FTE ($11.5M) in the adopted budget — 90 co-drivers, 52 drivers, 28 loaders, 11 foremen, etc. This is the single largest personnel change.
- **Office of the Mayor** lost 2 FTE
- **Office of Management and Budget** gained 3 FTE
- **Department of Human Resources** lost 1 FTE
- Many position title reclassifications (110 dropped titles, 158 added titles — most with $0 budget, suggesting renames not real additions)

### Capital Projects

| Metric | 2025 doc | 2026 doc |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Total projects | 67 | 64 |
| In both | 61 |
| Only in proposed | 6 |
| Only in adopted | 3 |

Projects dropped from proposed:
- Charles Anderson Bridge (TIP)
- Frankstown Avenue Signal Improvements (TIP)
- Liberty Avenue (HSIP)
- P.J. McArdle Roadway Bridge over Hillside (TIP)
- P.J. McArdle Roadway Bridge over Sycamore (TIP)

Projects added in adopted:

- Penn Avenue Reconstruction, Phase III (TIP)

Capital funding source data (`capital-funding-sources.json`) has per-project, per-year, per-source breakdowns that could show funding shifts.

### Revenue

Revenue waterfall data represents different budget years (2025 proposed year vs 2026 budget year), so it's **not directly comparable** for proposed-vs-adopted analysis. True revenue comparison would require extracting the "Revenue Detail" section's 2025 column from both documents at the line-item level.

### Budget Changes (within-year)

`budget-changes.json` has pre-computed department-level changes with FTE and budget for consecutive years. Both documents have this, with overlapping "2025 Budget" columns that could be compared at the department level — this is what the current page already does.

## Data Quality Issues

| Issue | Severity | Status |
|-------|----------|--------|
| >10x outlier filter is opaque | Low | Documented in footnote only |
| Department matching is by exact name | Medium | Any rename breaks match |
| Bureau of Environmental Services missing from one doc's dept-level view | Medium | May be structural (positions exist but no dept total row) |
| Revenue not comparable across docs | Medium | Different budget years in waterfall |
| Position reclassifications inflate add/drop counts | Low | Most $0-budget titles are renames |

## Deployment Gap

The deploy command in CLAUDE.md does not include the 2025 data build step. The cross-year page depends on `2025-department-budgets.json` being generated by:
```bash
python3 scripts/build_web_data.py --year 2025 --output-prefix 2025
```
This should be added to the deploy pipeline to prevent stale data.

## Decisions Needed

1. **Scope of expansion**: Should the cross-year page grow into a multi-tab deep-dive (personnel, capital, line items), or should these be separate pages?
2. **Position reclassification handling**: Should we try to fuzzy-match renamed positions, or present all adds/drops and let users interpret?
3. **Capital comparison scope**: Is project-level add/drop sufficient, or do we need funding-source-level comparison?
4. **Revenue comparison**: Is it worth extracting raw revenue line items for proposed-vs-adopted, or skip revenue?

## Recommendations

1. **Add personnel comparison** — the position-level data is rich and already available; this is the highest-value expansion
2. **Add capital project diff** — straightforward add/drop/rename analysis with minimal new data work
3. **Fix deploy pipeline** — add 2025 data build to deploy command
4. **Skip revenue comparison for now** — requires new extraction work for limited payoff
5. **Use tabs on the existing page** rather than creating separate pages — keeps the "proposed vs adopted" narrative coherent
6. **Filter out $0-budget position renames** to reduce noise in personnel comparison
